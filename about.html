Our Story

Built for owners who want the phone to ring, not more guesswork

MapClimb Marketing is a Local SEO and review removal agency for home service businesses and law firms across the US and Canada. The name says what we do: we help local businesses climb the map. It is one word, MapClimb. It was built around a simple idea: a local business should not have to guess whether its marketing is working. When a homeowner needs a roofer, a plumber or an HVAC company, they search on Google, look at the first few businesses on the map and call one. We want that call to be yours.

So we keep the work focused on what moves rankings and brings in calls: on page and off page SEO done together, a website built to rank and to turn visits into calls, and a clear report every month that shows where you rank, what moved and what we worked on.

We have built hundreds of SEO optimized websites for local businesses. Every one is built around the services a business offers and the areas it covers.
