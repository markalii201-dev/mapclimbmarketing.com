What people search

Pay for buyers, block the browsers

Google Ads charges for clicks, so every search you show for costs money. We sort searches by intent, bid on the ones that turn into jobs and block the ones that never will.

Ready to hire

Searches with the job and the urgency right in them. These get the strongest bids and an ad built around calling.

  • emergency plumber
  • ac repair near me
  • roof leak repair
  • garage door repair
  • water heater replacement

Comparing quotes

Bigger jobs with research behind them. We bid with care and send them to a page that answers cost questions.

  • new roof cost
  • furnace replacement quote
  • kitchen remodel contractor
  • fence installation estimate
  • panel upgrade cost

Do it yourself

People fixing it themselves. They click, read and leave, so we block these as negative keywords.

  • how to fix a leaky faucet
  • diy roof patch
  • how to reset a furnace
  • garage door spring kit

Jobs and supplies

Job seekers and parts shoppers. Paying for their clicks is pure waste, so they get blocked too.

  • plumber jobs
  • hvac technician salary
  • shingles for sale
  • electrician apprenticeship
  • water heater parts

Account settings

Settings that decide where the money goes

A few settings in a Google Ads account quietly decide who sees your ads. We set these from the start and check them every month.

  • Location

    People in your area

    We target people in or regularly in your towns, not people far away who only showed interest in them.

  • Schedule

    Hours that match

    Ads run when someone can answer. A midnight click that rings an empty office is money spent on nothing.

  • Networks

    Search first

    Display and search partner networks stay off unless we test them on purpose and they earn their place.

  • Tracking

    Calls, not clicks

    Calls from ads and the site, plus form fills, count as conversions, so bids follow real leads.

Common mistakes

Google Ads mistakes that burn budget

These show up in contractor ad accounts all the time, and each one pays for clicks that never call.

  • Broad match with no negatives

    Loose keywords show your ad for DIY questions, job searches and parts, and every one of those clicks is billed.

    What we do instead: A negative keyword list, updated from the search terms each week.

  • Ads shown outside your area

    The default location option can include people who live far away but searched about your area.

    What we do instead: Target people in or regularly in the towns you serve.

  • Every click lands on the home page

    Someone who searched water heater replacement has to hunt for it, and the call button is one more scroll away.

    What we do instead: A landing page for each campaign, matched to its ad.

  • Counting clicks, not calls

    Clicks and impressions look busy in a report but say nothing about booked jobs.

    What we do instead: Call and form conversion tracking, tested before launch.

  • Auto applied recommendations

    Google can apply its own suggestions, like higher budgets or broader keywords, without anyone on your side reviewing them.

    What we do instead: Recommendations reviewed by a person and applied only when they fit.
