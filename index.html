The Problem

The call goes to whoever shows up first on Google

Homeowners with a leak, a dead AC or a storm damaged roof do not dig through pages of results. They search, look at the first few businesses on the map and call one. If that is not you, the job goes to someone else.

  1. 01

    You are not in the top 3 on Google Maps.

    When someone nearby searches for what you do, the map shows three businesses first. Everyone below them has to hope the customer keeps scrolling.

  2. 02

    Your website gets visits but not calls.

    A slow site with thin service pages and no easy way to call sends people back to the search results, even when Google sends them straight to you.

  3. 03

    Your name online does not match your work.

    If your business does not look strong online, people pick the company that does, even when your work is better.
